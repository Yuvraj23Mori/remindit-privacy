# Privacy Policy for Remindit
**Last updated:** May 21, 2026

**1. Introduction**
At Dezdok, we are strictly committed to safeguarding your privacy while you use the Remindit application. We believe in transparency and data minimization. Our policy is straightforward: we do not sell your personal information, nor do we employ third-party advertising trackers. We only collect and process the data absolutely necessary to provide, secure, and improve your experience with our services.

**2. What Information We Collect and Why**
Remindit is an offline-first application. The tasks, times, and categories you create are stored locally on your device. 

To provide essential features, we use Firebase (a Google service) which collects:
* **Crash Reports & Basic Analytics:** Anonymous data (like your device model and operating system) to help us fix bugs and improve performance.
* **Push Notification Tokens:** A secure device ID used strictly to deliver your alarms and notifications on time.
* **Cloud Sync:** If enabled, your task data is securely backed up to our cloud database so you don't lose your reminders if you change phones. This data is encrypted in transit.

**3. Data Deletion**
You have complete control over your data. You can delete individual tasks within the app at any time. If you wish to completely wipe your synced cloud data and account information, please contact us at hello@dezdok.com and we will process your request immediately. 

**4. Children's Privacy**
Remindit does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.
